How to install

  1. Tap a download above in the Quest browser.
  2. Open the download in the Quest Files app (or pull it up on your PC and use adb install -r / SideQuest).
  3. Find the app under Library → Unknown Sources.
  4. Keep your Guardian boundary on. Room scan is not collision protection.
Installing over an older version keeps your high scores. Do not uninstall first.
